Individual Summer Research Project, Zhejiang University · Supervisor: Prof. Fei Miao

  • Combined MAPPO policy learning with local model predictive control in MPE2 to coordinate three communication-free agents covering three targets while reducing collisions.

  • Developed a local two-stage system that combines full-image foreground localization and instance splitting with batched segmentation of ear crops, producing per-ear abnormality masks and area ratios.

On a six-ear input, CPU dynamic batching increased abnormal-region segmenter throughput from 83.5 to 107.0 ears/s (1.28×); the complete in-memory pipeline averaged 234.5 ms.
